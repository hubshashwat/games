How To Play

📱 Touch / Mobile / Tablet:
• Stance: Drag horizontally on the bottom approach or use the Stance slider.
• Throw: Swipe up quickly towards the pins! The flick speed controls your ball velocity, and swipe angle controls direction.
• Curve / Hook: Adjust the Hook Spin slider to add left or right rotation for a pro curve into the 1-3 pocket!

💻 Laptop / Desktop:
• Mouse: Click and flick forward, or click ROLL BALL.
• Keyboard: Use [← / →] or [A / D] to position stance, [Q / E] to set hook spin, and [Spacebar] to roll!

🎳 Pro Bowling Tip:
For a strike, don't hit the headpin head-on! Curve the ball into the "pocket" between the 1-pin and 3-pin (for right-handers) at a 4° to 6° entry angle to trigger a domino chain reaction.

⭐ Scoring:
Standard USBC 10-frame rules. Strikes earn 10 + next 2 rolls. Spares earn 10 + next 1 roll. A perfect game is 300 points (12 consecutive strikes!).
